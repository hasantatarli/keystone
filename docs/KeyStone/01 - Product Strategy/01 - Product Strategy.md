# Purpose

> Why does Keystone exist?

Keystone exists to transform DataWiser's engineering knowledge into a reusable, standardized, and continuously evolving platform.

Rather than building software for the sake of software, Keystone captures proven engineering practices, operational experience, and automation into reusable capabilities.

These capabilities empower DataWiser consultants to deliver faster, more consistent, and higher-quality services while continuously improving through real customer engagements.

Keystone is not intended to replace engineering expertise.

Its purpose is to amplify engineering expertise.

---
# Core Philosophy

> What principles drive Keystone?

Keystone is built on engineering principles rather than specific technologies.

Database technologies evolve continuously. New platforms emerge, architectures change, and operational requirements shift over time.

Instead of building tools tightly coupled to a single database engine, Keystone focuses on reusable engineering capabilities that can be adapted across multiple data platforms.

Every capability within Keystone is designed to be:

- Knowledge-driven
- Technology-adaptive
- Repeatable
- Automated whenever possible
- Validated through real customer engagements

Keystone follows a consulting-first philosophy.

Capabilities are created to solve real engineering problems encountered by DataWiser consultants. As these capabilities mature through customer engagements, they evolve into reusable platform components.

Technology is the implementation detail.

Engineering is the product.

# Target Users

> Who is Keystone built for?

Keystone is designed with a layered user strategy.
### Primary Users
**DataWiser Engineers**

The primary users of Keystone are DataWiser engineers.

They use Keystone to perform assessments, execute operational tasks, automate repetitive work, and continuously improve engineering capabilities through real customer engagements.

Initially, this user group consists of the founder. As DataWiser grows, every engineer joining the company becomes both a user and a contributor to Keystone.
### Secondary Users
**Enterprise Customers**

Enterprise customers primarily consume the outputs generated by Keystone rather than interacting directly with the platform.

Typical outputs include:
- Health Assessment Reports
- Upgrade Assessments
- Architecture Reviews
- Operational Recommendations
- Capacity Reports
### Future Users

Organizations that do not engage DataWiser consulting services may use Keystone directly as a commercial platform.

In this scenario, Keystone serves database administrators, database reliability engineers, and engineering teams who wish to execute assessments and automation independently.

# Customer Problems

> Which real-world problems does Keystone solve?

DataWiser repeatedly encounters the same engineering challenges across different customers, database engines, and environments.

These problems are often solved manually despite following similar engineering processes.

Keystone exists to standardize these recurring activities.

Typical customer problems include:
- Unknown database health status
- Upgrade planning and risk assessment
- Configuration inconsistencies
- Performance degradation
- Capacity planning
- Backup verification
- High Availability validation
- Disaster Recovery readiness
- Security and compliance reviews
- Operational documentation gaps
The objective is not merely to automate technical tasks, but to standardize engineering knowledge and deliver consistent outcomes.

# Product Scope

## In Scope
- High-level performance indicators as part of Health Assessment
- Detailed problem-driven Performance Investigation as a separate engineering service
- Root cause analysis across database, infrastructure, and application interaction where relevant
- Optional remediation after root cause identification

## Out of Scope
At its initial stages, Keystone focuses exclusively on database engineering.

The platform intentionally excludes areas outside DataWiser's current expertise, including but not limited to:
- General system administration
- Enterprise storage management
- Network engineering
- Virtualization platforms
- General DevOps tooling
- Cloud infrastructure provisioning unrelated to database workloads
These areas may be revisited in the future as DataWiser expands its engineering capabilities.

Current focus is more important than future possibilities.

- Guaranteeing that every performance problem originates in the database layer
- Automatically applying changes outside DataWiser's responsibility or authorization
- Treating Health Assessment as a substitute for detailed performance investigation

# Commercial Philosophy

Keystone is not designed to generate engineering reports only.

Its purpose is to transform engineering findings into actionable implementation plans.

Every finding should support engineering prioritization, effort estimation, and customer proposal generation.

Commercial pricing remains a DataWiser business decision.

Keystone estimates engineering effort, while DataWiser determines commercial pricing based on its business model.
