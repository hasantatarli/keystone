# Keystone Vision

## Purpose

Keystone is the core engineering initiative of DataWiser.

Its purpose is to transform years of database engineering knowledge into a repeatable, automated and scalable operating system.

Instead of creating another database management product, Keystone aims to standardize how database services are assessed, automated and operated.

> **Keystone is not designed to replace monitoring platforms.**

> **Keystone is designed to transform monitoring data into engineering knowledge and repeatable actions.**

---

## Vision

To become the engineering foundation behind every DataWiser consulting engagement.

Every assessment, deployment, upgrade and operational task should be executable using Keystone's knowledge, automation and tooling.

---

## Principles

- Consulting first, product second.
- Solve real customer problems.
- Build reusable capabilities instead of isolated tools.
- Database agnostic whenever possible.
- Automation over manual work.
- Documentation before implementation.
- Simplicity before perfection.

---

## Long Term Goals

- Standardize database operations.
- Reduce repetitive engineering work.
- Build reusable capabilities.
- Continuously evolve through real customer engagements.
- Grow into the internal platform powering all DataWiser services.

---

## Success Criteria

Keystone is successful when:

- Every new customer engagement improves Keystone.
- Every improvement in Keystone improves future customer engagements.
- Knowledge becomes reusable.
- Operations become repeatable.
- Quality becomes consistent.
