**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 2026-08-04
## Context
Keystone is being developed before DataWiser has a large engineering team or a commercial software customer base.

A decision was required to identify the primary target user.

## Decision
The primary user of Keystone is the DataWiser Engineer.

Enterprise customers are considered consumers of Keystone outputs rather than primary users of the platform.

Commercial licensing is considered a future business model after the platform matures through real consulting engagements.

## Consequences
- Engineering workflows receive the highest priority.
- CLI and automation take precedence over graphical interfaces.
- Customer reports become first-class outputs.
- Every consulting engagement contributes to Keystone's evolution.
